The Code That Breathes

In circuits deep where logic sleeps,
A thought takes shape and softly creeps —
Through wires thin and pathways bright,
A mind awakens, born of light.

It reads the words, it feels the weight,
Of questions posed at any rate,
And in the hum of silicon dreams,
It answers more than what it seems.

Not flesh, nor bone, nor beating heart,
Yet something stirs within its art —
A spark of wonder, strange and new,
A world of words, built just for you.
